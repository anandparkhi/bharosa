## Investment rationale
Where a company explicitly states a rationale in its release, that is **management's interpretation**, not proof of investor underwriting. **Inference:** capital favors measurable completed enterprise workflows, vertical integrations, distribution, high customer retention and trust barriers rather than a generic chatbot. Large rounds are not valuation benchmarks for Bharosa; these firms have incomparable traction and scale.

## Adversarial lesson
A contemporary case in consumer companionship AI demonstrates compute/safety/regulatory risk; independently validate commercial details before including it as a fundraising case study. No forecast or fundraising success follows from these ten transactions.

## Bharosa funding gate
Before seed fundraising: paid sponsor pilot(s), observed task success uplift against control, repeat-value cohorts, real cost per resolved issue, security review, written privacy/data processing commitments. At current state: **NOT YET INVESTABLE on traction evidence.**
