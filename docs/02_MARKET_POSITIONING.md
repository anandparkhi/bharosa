# 02 — Market positioning
**Decision: Institution-sponsored digital task-completion assistance (B2B2C), starting with India. This is a strategic hypothesis, not proven demand.**

## One-line positioning
**Bharosa helps people complete unfamiliar digital tasks safely and independently, while giving service providers measurable reductions in unresolved customer friction.**

**User:** digitally uncertain adults, initially 50+ and first-time financial-app users, but not exclusively older people. **Economic buyer:** bank, insurer, telecom, housing/community service provider, employer. **Economic job:** fewer repeat calls, failed onboardings and costly branch/support visits. **Initial pilot:** digital tasks that do not trigger payment, impersonation, remote control or disclosure of credentials.

## Sector decision
Primary: **assisted digital service infrastructure / accessibility-first customer experience software**. Adjacent: AgeTech, consumer safety, guided service navigation. Avoid defining a new unvalidated 'scam AI' category.

| Sector | Customer pain | Recurrence | Payer and defensibility | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AgeTech | real, user context-specific | medium | household spend uncertain | beachhead |
| Consumer AI | broad but weak specific differentiation | high | intense platform competition | avoid horizontal play |
| Scam detection | severe but episodic | low | platform substitution strong | free trust gateway |
| Human concierge | valuable | high per user | proportional operating cost | capped optional tier |
| B2B2C assisted tasks | measurable support deflection | medium-high | contract and workflow integrations possible | **primary hypothesis** |
| AI agents moving money | severe liability | unknown | regulatory, auth and insurer risk | exclude |

## Buyer/user conflict
The institution pays but must not automatically see individual user activity. Use aggregate non-content usage only with independent informed consent. Institutional pilot must not claim 'bank-approved' without written permission.

## Geographic transfer
Tencent's WeChat Easy Mode and senior tutorial mini-program demonstrate embedded digital support inside high-frequency ecosystems: https://www.tencent.com/our-actions/social/making-technology-accessible/ . Transfer is **interface simplification + curated workflows**, not copying WeChat data access. India's uneven languages, OEM skins, open banking boundaries, and UPI PIN protection demand manual, least-privilege steps. U.S. vertical customer-experience agents show value in validated resolutions, but Bharosa should not copy their autonomous financial tools.

## Evidence context
TRAI dashboard reports 1,114.17 million internet subscriptions in June 2026 (subscriptions, not unique eligible users): https://trai.gov.in/ . NPCI lists 24,068.38 million UPI transactions in September 2026, which measures digital ecosystem scale, not Bharosa demand: https://www.npci.org.in/product/upi/product-statistics . Both figures must NOT be multiplied directly to infer paying customers.

## Pilot wedge
6 repeatable device tasks; privacy-first, English/Hindi/Marathi, offline-prepared guidance; built-in accessibility; verified safety redirects. Institution-specific workflows require authenticated published guidance, contracted service owner, and validated outcomes before selling.
