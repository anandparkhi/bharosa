## Five candidates for whitespace
1. **Completed task instead of AI conversation:** record outcome after each guided flow, with honest self-report distinction.
2. **India-language reviewed flows:** app/OEM-specific instructions with expiry/version ownership.
3. **Consent-based human help:** parent/user previews exactly what leaves the product, status distinct from actual delivery.
4. **Sponsored access without surveillance:** institution gets aggregated costs and outcomes, never raw message by default.
5. **Cross-service safety orchestration:** independently verified routes, clear abstention and no money-moving automation.

## Transfer analysis
China's embedded tutorials and easy modes transfer as design patterns; WeChat's platform privileges do not. US AI enterprise agent economics transfer as ROI measurement; their regulated account-action autonomy does not. India supports lower-cost curated guidance and browser speech, provided real device and language performance is measured.

## Strategic threat
All five whitespace ideas are **temporary hypotheses**. Big platforms may replicate commodity guidance; the durable advantage must become validated enterprise-specific workflows and distribution, plus observed trustworthy completion—not models, slogans or unconsented data accumulation.
