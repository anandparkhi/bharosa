# 05 — Bharosa business strategy
Decision date: 17 Oct 2026. All prices, demand and funnel metrics below are **hypotheses**.

## Model comparison (0-5; qualitative judgement, not validated data)
Weights: pain 20%, paid demand 20%, frequency 15%, scalable margin 15%, acquisition/distribution 15%, safety/regulatory feasibility 10%, defensibility 5%.
| Model | Pain | Pay | Frequency | Margins | Distribution | Safety | Defensibility | Score /100 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A Family subscription | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 56 |
| B B2B2C, institution pays | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | **76** |
| C Free safety + premium family | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 61 |
| D Pure fraud SDK / enterprise risk | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 66 |

B2B2C is the *best experiment* not proven market leader. Initial buyer: senior community or mid-tier financial institution with measurable digital-support costs; avoid lengthy bank procurement for first discovery. Free safety instructions remain publicly available. Do not sell the ability to report a fraud.

## Job and recurring user value
Recurring: installing and updating apps, finding settings, confirming linked accounts, recovering forgotten navigation, joining calls, learning a new digital service. Episodic: suspicious messages and recovery. The product's paid value must be observed assisted task completion and lower support effort, not avoidance anxiety.

## Monetisation hypothesis
Institutional license per eligible seat ₹25–₹50/month; baseline scenario ₹35, with a minimum contract floor to cover fixed cost to be negotiated (not counted in financial workbook). Optional trained-human support is capped and separately priced following cost observation. NO payment integration or actual contract exists.

## TAM / SAM / SOM — *illustrative channel-capacity hypotheses, not researched demand*
TAM exercise: 3,000 potential sponsors × 20,000 eligible seats × ₹35 × 12 = ₹2,520 crore annual *theoretical capacity*. SAM exercise: 150 realistically approached sponsor organisations × 15,000 addressable seats × ₹35 × 12 = ₹945 crore. SOM target illustration: 12 won partners × 10,000 billable seats × ₹35 × 12 = ₹50.4 crore annual at maturity. These are scenario inputs requiring actual channel lists, individual reachable seats and contract win-rate evidence; they are **not defensible current market-size facts or a three-year revenue promise**. TRAI and NPCI totals show digital activity, not willingness to pay.

## Business model risks
Procurement can take months, banks can build in-house, sponsor may want sensitive telemetry, 'avoided calls' needs controlled measurement. Institution cannot access private case content by default. User may decline sponsor telemetry and still use safety content. No automatic financial actions.

Measured business users: unknown. Revenue: unknown (do not infer zero from repo). ACV, CAC, gross margin, retention, willingness to pay: unknown. Tests and pricing panels to determine them are specified in 11_GO_TO_MARKET_AND_VALIDATION.md.
