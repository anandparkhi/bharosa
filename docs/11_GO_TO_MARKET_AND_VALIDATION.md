# 11 — GTM and commercial validation
No interviews, partners, paying users, subscriptions or marketing traction are claimed.

## First 100 users (30 days)
Recruit 20 senior-community volunteers, 30 adult caregivers and 50 digitally uncertain adults through Pune/Mumbai community associations or permissioned institutional outreach; avoid recruiting exclusively friends. Baseline ordinary task completion *without* Bharosa, then with Bharosa, six task types, balanced Android/iPhone and English/Hindi/Marathi. Budget hypothesis ₹15,000 for recruitment/travel/translations; confirm actual costs. Do not collect credentials/screenshots in research.

## 1,000 users (60–90 days)
Test community partner sponsorship and small business/service providers before bank procurement; three cohorts, at least 100 users each. Repeat value measured over weeks 2–4. No growth spend until guardrails and objective task outcomes pass.

## Validation experiments
| Experiment | Sample / duration | Goal hypothesis | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent-task completion | 60 tasks across two device families, 2 weeks | assisted success ≥20 percentage points above independent baseline | keep task if better with no critical unsafe instructions |
| Comprehension and trust | 24 adults across 3 languages, 2 weeks | ≥80% can explain limits and consent | revise copy if misses |
| Retention | 100 enrolled, 4 weeks | ≥25% repeat a valuable task | change task catalog or abandon repeat-value thesis |
| Scam safety | ≥300 labelled multilingual cases + harmless controls | zero catastrophic unsupported safe approvals in sample; report uncertainty interval | no expansion until red-team and privacy review |
| Willingness-to-pay | 20 procurement interviews, 4–8 weeks | ≥3 written requests for scoped paid pilots | stop enterprise build if no buyer interest |
| Paid proof | 2 paid pilots, 60–90 days | positive variable contribution and documented support-minutes reduction | no VC traction slide before contracts and measured data |
| Family sharing autonomy | 24 guided sessions | no unconsented data disclosure | product blocker if violated |

## Day 30 / 60 / 90 / 180
**30:** green CI, browser/screenreader review, 30 direct discovery interviews, 60 task observations, security fixes; must publish baseline and success distributions.
**60:** 100 consented cohort users, ≥25% week-2 valuable repeat hypothesis, at least 10 buyer calls and pilot proposal; finalize retention policy.
**90:** 300+ unique real users through targeted partners, 2 conditional pilot LOIs or written rejections, independently checked task outcomes; revenue remains zero until contracted.
**180:** aim for 2 paid institutional pilots, ≥1,000 engaged eligible users, gross margin measured from provider bills and staff time, evidence-led seed-decision meeting. If buy-side conversion absent, test family plan/vertical service or halt.
